# Reply to Cyrine — break excess / Philip Ore (2026-10-29)

---

Cyrine,

You were right about Philip Ore. The AED 120.99 is removed — it is no longer
in the October cycle, and nothing needs to be entered by hand.

**What was wrong.** On 16 Oct his record shows a 564-minute break inside a
580-minute attendance: he pressed Break Out 81 seconds before clocking out.
The engine already ignored a break that closed within 60 seconds of the
clock-out, so when he made exactly the same mistake on 2 Oct and closed it
9 seconds before leaving, that one was waived. The same man, the same button,
21 seconds apart, opposite outcomes.

**What it does now.** Break excess is only charged where the punches still
describe a break. Four shapes are read as a missed Break Out and are not
deducted:

- the break opens within a minute of the clock-in
- it closes within a minute of the clock-out
- it closes after the clock-out
- the day leaves under an hour of worked time while being credited a full shift

The fourth one is what catches Philip. October now has two break-excess
charges left, AED 2.47 and AED 2.71, both on days with about seven hours
actually worked — those are real.

**You can see this yourself from now on.** After you press Calculate, an amber
box lists every long break that was not charged, with the date, the minutes and
the reason. Those days still have a wrong punch record even though no money is
involved, so please correct them in the DTR.
