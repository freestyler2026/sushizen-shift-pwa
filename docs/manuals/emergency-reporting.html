When something happens at your store

How to tell HQ, in about two minutes.

You will never be in trouble for reporting something.

Not even if it turns out to be small. The only thing that causes a problem is when nobody hears about it. If you are unsure — report it.

Open Report Something

It is in the menu. You will see two choices.

🚨
It is happening now — we need help
No water. No power. Gas. A fire. Someone hurt. A freezer that stopped. A storm. Anything that is going on right now.
📄
It already happened — for the record
Something to look into so it does not happen again. A product problem, a delivery, stock, a customer.

Not sure which one? Use the first. It is always the safer choice.

You do not have to decide how serious it is

You will not be asked whether it is an emergency, how bad it is, or whose fault it is. Pick what it is, and the system works out the rest.

What you pickWhat it becomes
Fire · Injury · Flood · Forced to closeLevel 3 HQ told at once, every 10 minutes until someone answers
Water · Electricity · Gas · SecurityLevel 2 HQ told at once, every 30 minutes until someone answers
Equipment · Weather · BuildingLevel depends on your answer to “Is trading affected?”

What it asks you

Six things. Two of them are optional.

  • What is it? — choose from the list
  • Which store?
  • When did you notice? — not when it started. When you saw it
  • Is trading affected? — Yes / Not yet
  • Can the store keep going? — Yes / Limited / No
  • Do you need HQ to do something? — Yes / No

The last two questions are what let HQ decide whether to send water from the central kitchen, arrange a tank, or change how the store trades. Without them they can only guess.

Photos and the full story come later. Send the short report first. You can add detail once things have settled.

Why the time matters so much

The sooner HQ knows, the more they can do.
Told at 2pm
Water can be sent from the central kitchen. A tank can be arranged. Trading can be changed.
Told at 7pm
There is almost nothing left to try.

This is the whole reason the fast report exists. Nobody can stop the building’s water going off — but the hours after you notice are the part we control.

Aim for ten minutes. From the moment you notice, to the moment you send. That is all it takes, and it is the one thing that decides whether HQ can help.

What happens after you press send

HQ is told straight away
Not at the end of the day. The moment you send it.
If nobody answers, it asks again
Every 10 minutes for Level 3, every 30 for Level 2. It does not go quiet.
Then it starts naming people
After two rounds with no answer, it asks one person at a time by name, working through HQ until somebody replies.
Someone takes it on
Their name goes on your report and the alerts stop. From then on it is theirs, not yours.

You do not need to chase it. That is the system’s job now.

For a fire or an injury — call first

Nobody opens an app during a fire. Call, get people safe, then send the report when you can. The report is the record — it is not how you raise the alarm when someone is in danger.

One thing to do today

Open My Phone Number and add your number. Start with the country code, like +63 917 123 4567.

It takes a few seconds, and only you can do it — nobody can enter it for you. It is used to reach you about an emergency at a store, and for nothing else. Only HQ and HR can see it.

Questions people ask

What if I report something and it turns out to be nothing?

That is fine, and it always will be. Nobody counts how many reports a store sends, on purpose — because counting them would teach everyone to send fewer.

What if my manager is not around?

Send it yourself. Anyone can. The rule is that whoever notices reports it — so that nobody ends up waiting for somebody else to do it.

What if someone already reported it?

Send it anyway. Two reports of the same thing costs nothing. Zero reports costs a day of trading.

It is not our fault — the building did it. Should I still report?

Yes, and this is the important one. A report is not only about blame or fixing a cause. When the problem is outside our control, the report is what gives HQ time to work around it.

Do I need to write a lot?

No. Six answers, most of them a single tap. The written part is optional.
