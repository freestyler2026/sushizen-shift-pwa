Time In — confirming you are in the store

From 5 September, clocking in has one extra step: confirm you are inside the store. It takes about five seconds. Your time in is never blocked — if the code will not scan, you still clock in and confirm another way.

What to do

  1. Clock in as usual

    Open the OS and press Time In. Nothing has changed here.

  2. Scan the QR poster inside the store

    Point your normal phone camera at the poster — the same camera you use for photos. A link appears on screen. Tap it. That is all. You do not need to open the app first, and there is no scanner to install.

  3. If the code will not scan — send a photo instead

    Take one photo with the store visible behind you and upload it in the app. This is the backup only. Use it when the poster is missing, damaged, or your camera will not read it.

You can confirm at any time during your shift

If your camera fails at 7:00, scan at 9:00 — it still counts for the whole day. There is no deadline and nothing to rush. Confirming later is exactly as good as confirming at the start.

The three states

Confirmed
You scanned the QR
Nothing further. This is the normal case.
Photo sent
You sent a photo instead
The back office will look at it. Fine to use when the code will not scan.
Not confirmed
Neither was done
Shown on your own screen so you can fix it, and listed for the back office.

Why this exists

GPS can tell that your phone was near the store. It cannot tell whether you came inside. The poster is placed where only someone in the store can reach it, so scanning it answers the question GPS cannot.

Working from home

Back office staff approved to work from home do not scan anything. This step does not apply to you and your time in is unaffected.

If something goes wrong

Clock in first, always. Your time in is never blocked by this step, so never delay it to sort the code out. Confirm afterwards, or send the photo. If the poster is missing or damaged, tell your manager so it can be replaced — but clock in first.

QR posters — one per site

For HQ and management — not for circulation

This page holds every site's code. Cut them apart and send each site only its own. Do not share this page with store staff, and do not post it in a group chat: a code that everyone already has on their phone is not evidence that anyone walked into a store.

Where to post it: inside the store, where it cannot be read from the doorway, through a window, or from the pavement. The kitchen or back-of-house wall is right. Cubao and the Manila Central Kitchen share one poster — same kitchen.
