How this was checked

調べ方

Every product sold in the last 30 days was matched to Cost Calculation by name, then through pos_item_cost_map for the names the master spells differently. Separately, all 161 active menu items that carry a selling price had their cost recomputed and compared against it.

直近30日に売れた全商品を Cost Calculation と品名で突き合わせ、表記が違うものは pos_item_cost_map を通しました。別途、売価を持つ有効な 161品すべての原価を再計算して売価と比べています。

After the two fixes, the whole menu was measured again — 161 priced items, median 33.1%, highest 70.4%, none above 100%. Six items still read 58–70%; they are the beverages and condiments in the section above, and their material prices were checked one by one.
2件の修正後、全品を再測しました — 売価のある161品、中央値 33.1%、 最高 70.4%、100%超はゼロ。58〜70%が6品残りますが、 これは上のセクションの飲料・調味料で、材料単価は1つずつ確認済みです。
What this method cannot see: a component item with no selling price of its own — exactly what Chicken Karaage 3pcs is. Those were checked separately by cost ranking, and it was the most expensive of them by a wide margin. If a component is quietly wrong and cheap, this sweep would still miss it.
この方法が見られない範囲: 売価を持たない中間品。 Chicken Karaage 3pcs がまさにそれです。中間品は原価の高い順に別途確認し、 これが突出して1位でした。ただし安いまま静かに誤っている中間品は、 この走査では今も見つけられません。
