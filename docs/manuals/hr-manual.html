🆕 Start Here — Two Ways to Make an NTE

Both routes offer the same ready-made wording. What differs is who may use them, and how many steps stand between you and the employee.

① NTE Request ② Create Notice
Who can use it Anyone with page access HQ / ADMIN / HR Manager only
Templates ✓ 161 violations ✓ Same 161 violations

⚠️ Nothing is added after you submit. Load the wording BEFORE submitting, with the Select violation button.

The same violation list is now on BOTH routes. The steps are identical either way.

🆕 HR — Handling a Request That Came In

A request never reaches the employee by itself. It stops twice, and each stop needs a person.

PENDING→ Approve → APPROVED→ Issue → ISSUED
  1. 1
    Open Pending and read the request

    Check name, date, reason and any attached photo.

  2. 2
    Approve or Reject

    Approve → Pending Issuance. Reject closes it with a note.

  3. 3
    Issue it — this is the step people forget

    Approved is NOT sent. Until you click Issue, the employee sees nothing.

💡 Before issuing, check no ________ remains and that the violation code matches the text. Otherwise reject with a note.
🆕 Requests From Both Cities, and What Happens If One Stalls

Changed 28 Aug 2026. The list used to show only the city tab you were on.

  1. 1
    HR now sees Manila and Dubai requests together

    Both cities appear in Pending and Pending Issuance. Only the request queue is shared.

  2. 2
    A blue city tag marks requests from the other city

    A DUBAI tag on the Manila tab means that request is Dubai's. You can still approve it.

  3. 3
    After 2 days it appears in Waiting for Someone

    Each stage is timed separately. Past 7 days it turns red.

💡 Review-waiting items have an Approve button; issuance-waiting items only link to the record. Issuing sends a formal notice, so it belongs on the page where you can read the full reason.

Issue New NTE — 3-Step Wizard

Click "+ Issue New NTE" top right. Complete 3 steps, then a second admin approves and serves (4-eyes).

  1. 1
    Staff & Violation

    Select employee, pick a violation. System auto-sets response days (PH: 5 days minimum).

  2. 2
    Incident Details

    Describe incident (min 120 chars), impact (min 60 chars). Add ≥2 evidence when no witnesses.

  3. 3
    Review & Submit for Approval

    Click "Submit NTE for Approval" — system creates IR, generates draft (Approval Pending). A second admin then Approves and Serves.

Draft 1st Review 2nd Review 3rd Review Finalized Email Sent Payment Done
Creating a Clearance Case
  1. 1
    Click New Clearance Case

    Fill in city, separation type, employee info, and last working day.

  2. 2
    Case is created as Draft

    Open the case to begin computation and advance through stages.

Advancing Through Stages

Each stage must be advanced by the correct designated reviewer. The system records whoever is logged in when Advance is clicked.

  1. 1
    Open the case

    Click the employee row in the list.

  2. 2
    Check computation and enter optional notes

    Review Final Pay figures. Add notes if needed.

  3. 3
    Click Advance

    For Stages 1–3, a confirmation banner appears first.

    System confirmation banner (example)
    Confirm advance?
    Ayako Nishimura will be recorded as the 3rd Review approver.
  4. 4
    Verify the name shown, then click Yes, advance

    If the wrong person is shown, cancel and have the correct reviewer log in first.

  5. 5
    Stage 4 (Finalized) → Mark Email Sent

    Enter email and click Mark Email Sent after sending the statement.

  6. 6
    Stage 5 (Email Sent) → Mark Payment Done

    Click Mark Payment Done once payment is released.

Important: Who Gets Recorded as Approver

The system records the currently logged-in user as the approver — whoever clicks the button, regardless of who was designated.

StageDesignated ReviewerRule

⚠️ If you advanced as the wrong person, use Return to Draft immediately. This resets all stage history — all reviewers must re-advance from the beginning.

Policy Documents
Admin → HR Policy Documents  |  Staff → Company Policies
Upload company policies as PDFs. Staff acknowledge receipt with PIN. HR tracks completion.
Admin: Uploading a Policy Document

HQ / Admin / HR Manager / Manila Management roles can upload. Max 10 MB, PDF only.

  1. 1
    Open HR Policy Documents

    Navigate to Admin → HR Policy Documents in the sidebar.

  2. 2
    Click Upload Document

    A modal opens with the following fields:

    • Title — shown to staff (required)
    • Category — Policy / Memo / Announcement / Guideline / SOP
    • City — All / Manila / Dubai (controls visibility)
    • Description — optional summary
    • Effective Date (optional)
    • Requires Acknowledgement toggle
    • Acknowledgement Deadline (if required)
    • PDF File (required)
  3. 3
    Click Upload — the document is immediately visible to staff.
Admin: Tracking Acknowledgements
  1. 1
    Click View Acknowledgements on any document card.

    A panel shows every staff member who acknowledged, with date/time.

  2. 2
    Compare the list against your full staff list to find who hasn't acknowledged yet.

    Non-acknowledgers are absent from the list; their portal shows an Amber warning.

  3. 3
    Archive or Delete when no longer needed.

    Archive hides from staff but keeps history. Delete is permanent.

Staff: Viewing and Acknowledging a Policy

All staff can access Company Policies from the sidebar.

  1. 1
    Open Company Policies from the sidebar.

    Amber ⚠️ = pending acknowledgement. Red = overdue deadline.

  2. 2
    Click View PDF or Download to read the document.
  3. 3
    Click Acknowledge to confirm receipt.

    A 2-step confirmation modal appears:

    • Step 1 — Read the declaration and click I Understand — Continue.
    • Step 2 — Enter your PIN to sign.

    After confirmation, a green ✓ Acknowledged badge appears with the date.
