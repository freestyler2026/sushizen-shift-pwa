HR Clearance
Admin → HR Clearance
Formal final pay computation with multi-stage approval before payment.
7-Stage Approval Workflow
Draft 1st Review 2nd Review 3rd Review Finalized Email Sent Payment Done
Creating a Clearance Case
  1. 1
    Click New Clearance Case

    Fill in city, separation type, employee info, and last working day.

  2. 2
    Case is created as Draft

    Open the case to begin computation and advance through stages.

Advancing Through Stages

Each stage must be advanced by the correct designated reviewer. The system records whoever is logged in when Advance is clicked.

  1. 1
    Open the case

    Click the employee row in the list.

  2. 2
    Check computation and enter optional notes

    Review Final Pay figures. Add notes if needed.

  3. 3
    Click Advance

    For Stages 1–3, a confirmation banner appears first.

    System confirmation banner (example)
    Confirm advance?
    Ayako Nishimura will be recorded as the 3rd Review approver.
  4. 4
    Verify the name shown, then click Yes, advance

    If the wrong person is shown, cancel and have the correct reviewer log in first.

  5. 5
    Stage 4 (Finalized) → Mark Email Sent

    Enter email and click Mark Email Sent after sending the statement.

  6. 6
    Stage 5 (Email Sent) → Mark Payment Done

    Click Mark Payment Done once payment is released.

Important: Who Gets Recorded as Approver

The system records the currently logged-in user as the approver — whoever clicks the button, regardless of who was designated.

StageDesignated ReviewerRule
1st ReviewHR / Computation staffMust be logged in as that person
2nd ReviewHR / Computation staffMust be logged in as that person
3rd ReviewSenior manager / designated approverMust be logged in as that person
FinalizedHQ / AdminMust be logged in as that person
⚠️ If you advanced as the wrong person, use Return to Draft immediately. This resets all stage history — all reviewers must re-advance from the beginning.
