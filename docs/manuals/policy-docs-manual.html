Company Policies — Staff Guide
Policy Document Hub
The Company Policies page is where Sushi ZEN HR publishes official policies, memos, SOPs, and announcements as PDFs. You can read them inside the app and confirm receipt with your PIN — no more paper signatures.
✨ What this feature does
For StaffFor HR / Managers
View all active company policies and memos Upload policy PDFs (max 10 MB)
Preview or download PDFs in the app See who has and hasn't acknowledged
Confirm receipt with your PIN Set acknowledgement deadlines
See your acknowledgement status Archive documents when no longer active
💡 Acknowledgement is a permanent digital record. Once you confirm with your PIN, the timestamp is stored and cannot be removed.

Viewing a PDF
You can read any policy document directly in the app, or download it to your device.
👁️ Option A: View PDF in-browser
  1. 1
    Click "View PDF" on any document card

    The PDF opens in a full-screen overlay inside the app.

  2. 2
    Click "Download" in the viewer toolbar for a copy
  3. 3
    Click ✕ to close and return to the list
⬇️ Option B: Download to device
  1. 1
    Click "Download" on a document card

    The PDF saves to your device's download folder.

⚠️ Viewing or downloading a PDF does NOT count as acknowledgement. You must click Acknowledge and enter your PIN separately.

Acknowledging a Policy
When HR requires acknowledgement, you'll see an Acknowledge button. This is a 2-step process.
1️⃣ Step 1 — Read the declaration
  1. 1
    Click the purple "Acknowledge" button

    A modal opens titled "Acknowledge Document".

  2. 2
    Read the document summary and deadline in the modal
  3. 3
    Read the declaration, then click "I Understand — Continue"

    This confirms you have received and understood the policy.

After Acknowledging
Once your PIN is verified, the acknowledgement is recorded instantly.
✨ What changes on your screen
  1. 1
    The modal closes automatically
  2. 2
    The document card shows a green ✓ "Acknowledged" badge with today's date
  3. 3
    "Acknowledge" button disappears — only View PDF and Download remain
  4. 4
    KPI cards update: Acknowledged ↑, Pending ↓
✓ Your HR team can now see that you have acknowledged. The timestamp and your name are permanently recorded.
⚠️ Important notes
🔒 Acknowledgement is permanent. Once confirmed, it cannot be undone or removed — not by you, not by HR.
👤 Your PIN = your signature. Never enter your PIN on behalf of another staff member.
🔁 Each document is acknowledged once. Once confirmed, you cannot acknowledge it again.

Frequently Asked Questions
Common questions from staff about the Policy Documents feature.
💬 Q & A
I forgot my PIN — can I still acknowledge? No. Ask your manager or HR to reset your PIN first, then acknowledge.
I clicked Acknowledge by mistake — can I cancel? Yes — click Cancel or ✕ before entering your PIN. Once confirmed it cannot be reversed.
Do I need to acknowledge every document? Only documents with an Acknowledge button. Informational-only documents need no action.
I don't see a document my colleague sees. Why? Some documents are city-specific. If it is not for your city, it won't appear on your page.
Can I read the PDF after I acknowledge? Yes — View PDF and Download are always available after acknowledging.
My screen shows a red Overdue badge. What should I do? Acknowledge it immediately. Read the document first, then confirm with your PIN. Contact HR if you have questions.

HR Policy Documents — Admin Guide
Uploading Documents
HR Managers, HQ, and Admin can upload PDFs up to 10 MB from the admin page.
📤 Upload steps
  1. 1
    Go to Admin → HR Policy Documents, click "+ Upload Document"

    The upload modal opens.

  2. 2
    Fill in the required fields
    • Title (required) — shown to staff
    • Category — Policy / Memo / Announcement / Guideline / SOP
    • Target City — All / Manila / Dubai
    • Description (optional)
    • Effective Date (optional)
    • Require acknowledgement toggle
    • Acknowledgement Deadline (when toggle is ON)
    • PDF File (required, max 10 MB)
  3. 3
    Click "Upload" — the document is immediately visible to staff

    KPI cards update and staff can see the document immediately.

⚠️ Documents publish to staff immediately. There is no draft mode — upload only when ready.

Tracking Acknowledgements
See exactly who confirmed and when, so you know who needs follow-up.
🔍 Viewing the acknowledgement report
  1. 1
    Click on a document card to expand it

    Action buttons appear: Download PDF, View Acknowledgements, Archive, Delete.

  2. 2
    Click "View Acknowledgements (N)"

    A panel shows the document title, total count, deadline, and every staff member who acknowledged with their timestamp.

  3. 3
    Compare against your full staff list to find who hasn't acknowledged yet

    Non-acknowledgers are absent from the list. Their portal shows a pending or overdue badge.

Archiving & Restoring
Archive an outdated policy to hide it from staff while keeping history intact.
📦 Archive a document
  1. 1
    Click the document card to expand it
  2. 2
    Click the amber "Archive" button

    The document disappears from staff's view immediately. KPI cards update.

♻️ Restore an archived document
  1. 1
    Enable "Show Archived" checkbox near the filter bar

    Archived documents appear with an "Archived" badge.

  2. 2
    Click "Restore" on the document

    The document returns to the active list and becomes visible to staff again.

🗑️ Delete a document (permanent)

Deleting permanently removes the document and all acknowledgement records. This cannot be undone.

  1. 1
    Expand the card and click the red Delete button
  2. 2
    Confirm the deletion in the browser dialog

    Document and all acknowledgement history are permanently removed.

⚠️ Prefer Archive over Delete. Archiving preserves acknowledgement history. Delete only for documents uploaded by mistake.
