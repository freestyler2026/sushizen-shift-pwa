Closing your work

Five screens hand you a list. Each one needs you to close the item when you are done with it. This shows exactly where the button is, and when to press it.

Why this guide exists

Almost nothing is being closed. Of the last 79 back-office tasks, 62 were removed by the system because they expired or were swept up — not because anyone finished them.

That is not a complaint about you. Until last week the screen did not even show the items waiting to be closed, so there was no way to know. That is fixed. This guide is the other half — showing where the button is.

Closing does not mean "I solved it". It means "this no longer needs anyone's attention." An item that turned out to be nothing still has to be closed. An item that is now too old to act on still has to be closed. Leaving it open does not keep it alive — it just hides the ones that do need you.

1 · Back Office tasks

Admin → Store Operations → BO Dashboard

Each task goes through the same three steps: you send it to the store manager, the manager replies, and you read the reply and close it. Most items get stuck at the third step, so watch for the green label.

Take over Read reply & close

The green Reply in — read & close label is the one that needs you. Read what the manager wrote, then press the green button.

When you press it you choose one of four:

ChooseWhen
HandledThe manager's answer settles it. Most common.
No action neededIt should not have been raised — wrong branch, already fine, duplicate.
Couldn't resolveSomebody tried and it cannot be fixed.
Too lateThe moment passed. A rush check from three days ago cannot be done now. Use this instead of leaving it open.
"Too late" is a correct answer, not an admission. It was added because there was no honest option for an item that had simply aged out, so those rows sat open forever and buried the ones that mattered.

Take over is separate. Every task has an owner. If the owner is someone else and you are the one handling it, press Take over first so the record matches who actually did it.

Resolve once the supplier has given a credit note or a replacement delivery — and also when you have checked and there is nothing to claim.

Work through one supplier at a time, not one claim at a time. Open everything for that supplier, make one call, then resolve them together.

If you are unsure

  • Do not close what you have not decided. On Procurement alerts use Mark Reviewed; on Prep Time use Cannot tell. Both keep the item visible.
  • Everything can be undone. Prep Time has an Undo next to the row. A closed Back Office task can be reopened.
  • If a button does nothing, or the screen says something that does not match what you see, tell us. That is our fault, not yours — and the last three problems in the Backup Report were found exactly that way.
